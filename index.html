How this works and what it misses

Populated areas are the residential zones mapped in OpenStreetMap, plus any village, town or city point, plus any cluster of buildings that looks like an unmapped village. Buildings inside a mapped zone are never downloaded, which keeps scans small. Everything else is a lone house.

Unmapped homes do not appear, and untagged buildings include barns and sheds, so tick the box above off for a stricter picture. Distances are straight lines. Check the satellite view before you trust any spot, and respect land access.

Each scan covers your chosen radius around the map centre, plus a mile and a half margin so the edges stay accurate. It loads in small parts, and smaller radii load faster.
