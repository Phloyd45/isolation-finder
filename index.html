How this works and what it misses

Populated areas are the residential zones mapped in OpenStreetMap, plus any village, town or city point, plus any cluster of buildings that looks like an unmapped village. Everything else is a lone house.

Unmapped homes do not appear, and untagged buildings include barns and sheds, so tick the box above off for a stricter picture. Distances are straight lines. Check the satellite view before you trust any spot, and respect land access.

Inside Great Britain the map uses ready-made data built each month from OpenStreetMap. Anywhere else it asks the live OpenStreetMap server, which is slower.

Set a radius to zero to ignore that kind of place. Each scan covers your search radius around the map centre, plus a margin as wide as your largest radius so the edges stay accurate. Areas you scan are saved on this device for 30 days, so scanning the same or a nearby area again only downloads what is missing.
